## The honest advice, worth keeping at the bottom of this file

You get better from here by building things you want to exist, looking up what you do not
know as you need it, and reading error messages carefully.

Do not work through another twelve-week syllabus. You are past the stage where structured
teaching is the fastest route, and tutorials feel productive in a way that building does
not, which is exactly the trap.

Pick something slightly too hard, start it, and get stuck. The getting stuck is the
learning. Everything you found hard in the last twelve weeks you now find easy, and that
happened by being stuck and then not being stuck.

And keep the course repo. It is a reference written in your own words, about your own
confusions, which is better than any documentation.
