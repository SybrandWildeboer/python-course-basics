How to read this page

The name of a notebook says what it is for

A walkthrough notebook has no suffix: it is the session's material, to run cell by cell. -exercise and -homework notebooks are yours to fill in. -solved notebooks are the answers, for after you have tried. The -stretch-exercise notebooks are optional extra exercises for when a session runs early, and -stretch-solved has their worked answers.

Open a notebook in VS Code: open the repository folder, then the path shown here, and pick the Python kernel at the top right. The browser cannot run a notebook, which is why the paths below are not links.

The run sheet is each session's README.md: timings, what to watch out for, and the numbers worth knowing. It is written for whoever teaches, and is worth reading as a learner too.
