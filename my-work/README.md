# my-work

Your scratch space. Nothing in here is ever checked, graded or looked at unless you ask.

Use it for:

- trying something out before you put it in a real exercise file,
- notes to yourself,
- the half-finished thing you want to come back to.

The exercises and homework you are asked to hand in live in
`sessions/session-NN/exercises/` and `sessions/session-NN/homework/` instead.
